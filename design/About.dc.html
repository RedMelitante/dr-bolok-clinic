About Dr. Vira

Trusted Newborn & Child Care, Close to Home

Dr. Viraliza June Abejuela Bolok, MD has dedicated her career to the health and development of children — from their very first moments of life through their growing years. As a board-certified pediatrician with a sub-specialty in neonatology, she brings hospital-level expertise in newborn and high-risk infant care to every family she sees, paired with the attentiveness of a personal pediatrician.

Newborn & High-Risk Infant Care Specialized training in neonatology means Dr. Vira is equipped to care for premature and high-risk newborns, not just routine checkups.
Trusted Across Multiple Hospitals Patients can see Dr. Vira at Capitol University Medical Center, Maria Reyna–Xavier University Hospital, or J.R. Borja General Hospital — whichever is most convenient.
