About Dr. Vira

A Dedicated Partner in Your Child’s Health

Dr. Viraliza June Abejuela Bolok, MD is a licensed physician (Physician Licensure Examination, 2014) and a board-certified pediatrician with a sub-specialty in neonatology, certified through the Philippine Pediatric Society. For over a decade, she has cared for newborns and children across Cagayan de Oro, combining hospital-level neonatal expertise with the warmth of a family pediatrician.

She currently sees patients at Capitol University Medical Center, Maria Reyna – Xavier University Hospital, and serves as Medical Specialist IV in Pediatrics at J.R. Borja General Hospital.

  • Licensed physician since 2014
  • Board-certified Pediatrician, Philippine Pediatric Society
  • Sub-specialty in Neonatology (newborn & premature infant care)
  • Medical Specialist IV, Pediatrics – J.R. Borja General Hospital
  • Consulting Pediatrician – Capitol University Medical Center & Maria Reyna – Xavier University Hospital
